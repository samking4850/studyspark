# Student walkthrough for judges

Open https://studyspark-orcin.vercel.app/ and choose **Experience the demo**.

1. **Student signup:** use the sample name and school email. Explain that colleges would provide access; this creates only a demo identity.
2. **Personalize:** answer all 11 questions. For a visible example, choose detailed explanations, scenario-based challenges, help unpacking course readings, plain-language short sections, sentence starters, and 15 minutes.
3. **Join a class:** join FIN101. Point out the instructor objectives and sample materials.
4. **Start learning:** review “What changes in this lesson?” and open a lesson. Show the foundation bridge, detailed explanation, scenario practice, and writing support. Optional: take the starting-point check to demonstrate topic prioritization.
5. **Class study event:** create an event and preview an invitation. Play Word Wheel: spin, guess letters, and solve COMPOUND INTEREST. Edit the event to try Category Quest: answer across finance topics and review missed topics. Classmates and invitations are simulated; no messages are sent.
6. **Why StudySpark:** explain the proposed solution: colleges connect instructor goals to individual support, targeted practice, and class participation. Competitors already offer adaptive practice and games; our combined workflow is a hypothesis to validate with colleges, not proven exclusivity.

The walkthrough is prepared sample content. Live school sign-in, Canvas enrollment/rosters, actual invitation delivery, cloud student records, and grounded AI generation remain future work. Reading comfort is a self-report, not a measured reading level.
